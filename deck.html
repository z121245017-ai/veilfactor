01 · THE REAL-WORLD PROBLEM

Why Trade Finance Cannot Prevent Double-Pledging on Public Chains

The Double-Financing Crisis

Suppliers routinely pledge the exact same commercial receivable to Factor A and Factor B simultaneously (multi-billion dollar fraud class seen in Greensill and commodity trade finance).

Competing lenders refuse to pool invoice registries because sharing buyer names, SKU pricing, and credit limits reveals proprietary commercial relationships.

Why Traditional Blockchains Fail

Public EVM ledgers expose invoice amounts, counterparties, and supplier liquidity distress to competitors and front-runners.

Private silos hide data but fail to stop a rogue supplier from financing the same invoice at a second desk.

Midnight solves the exact paradox: global cryptographic uniqueness with programmable data protection.

02 · MIDNIGHT DUAL-LEDGER ARCHITECTURE

Strict Separation of Private Local Witnesses & Public State

Private Local Prover (Witnesses — NEVER Disclosed)

  • localInvoiceDocHash() — SHA-256 of commercial invoice PDF
  • localBuyerTaxHash() — Buyer corporate tax identifier
  • localFaceValueCents() — Exact commercial invoice value
  • localBuyerExposureCents() — Existing portfolio concentration
  • localCreditScore() — Exact supplier/buyer credit score
  • localSecretKey() & localAuditorSecretKey()

Public Midnight Ledger (Zero-Leakage Commitments)

  • pledgedNullifiers: Set<Bytes<32>> — Global double-pledge lock independent of wallet or salt
  • deals: Map<Bytes<32>, DealSummary> — Salted commitment, auditor seal, coarse RiskTier & AmountBand
  • Aggregate protocol counters (totalPledged, totalFunded, totalSettled, totalAuditorDisclosures)

03 · COMPILED COMPACT 0.36.1 CIRCUIT SUITE

6 Provable Circuits · End-to-End Multi-Party Lifecycle

1. pledgeInvoice()

Proves credit score floor, buyer concentration cap, Tier-C ≤80% advance guardrail, and deterministic anti-double-pledge nullifier uniqueness.

2. fundDeal()

Authenticates designated factor via domain-separated derived PK; locks deal into FUNDED state.

3. settleDeal()

Closes deal on buyer repayment while permanently retaining spent nullifier to block replay financing.

4. flagDispute()

Bilateral freeze callable by either supplier or factor; halts settlement pending compliance audit.

5. attestSelectiveDisclosure()

Designated auditor proves exact off-chain commercial dossier matches on-chain disclosureSeal without publishing raw figures.

6. updateRiskPolicy()

Protocol admin governance circuit tightening global credit floor, max advance bps, and concentration cap.
